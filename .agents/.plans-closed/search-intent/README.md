## Measurement

Use Google Search Console query/page exports as the outcome metric; repository
tests can prove markup and links, but not ranking or CTR.

- Record a pre-launch 28-day baseline by query and page for the npm-intent cluster,
  `svelte-tiny-virtual-list`, and `/examples`.
- At day 14, check indexing and query-to-page assignment. Do not rewrite copy yet
  unless the wrong URL is ranking or a page is excluded from indexing.
- At day 28, compare clicks, impressions, CTR, and average position against the
  baseline. Keep query definitions fixed so the comparison is meaningful.
- Primary success signals: npm-cluster CTR becomes non-zero and `/install` becomes
  the dominant landing page; the competitor page enters positions 1–10;
  `/examples` CTR improves without losing material impressions.
